## Honest contingencies

- No applicable warning at the saved point: show the actual empty/current feed and its natural recommendation. Then open the captured closure record in REPLAY and say, “This warning was captured earlier; it is not live now.” Do not silently move the office, alter thresholds or insert a synthetic alert.
- Unsupported hazard: show review requirement. The acceptance override reason can say: “Prototype test: retain protective candidate; unsupported categories and local access require supervisor review. Synthetic roster, drafts only.” This does not claim a building inspection or authorize real employees.
- AI degraded: show that status and actual successful tool events if present. Say, “The AI selection failed validation; the deterministic evidence remains available.” Do not describe the historical active AI brief as the current response.
- Slow provider/run: explain the visible loading state. If time requires switching to historical evidence, explicitly announce the switch. An already-started evaluation may continue independently; replay itself does not initiate new source/model work.
- API/source failure: show incomplete/error and remaining inputs. Do not manufacture normal, change the expected result, or claim the live segment completed successfully.
